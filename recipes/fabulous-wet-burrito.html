Fabulous Wet Burrito

Back to Homepage

Description

These wet burritos are very easy, yet very good. I got this recipe from a friend, and everyone I serve them to asks for the recipe. Serve with guacamole, sour cream, and salsa.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • ½ cup chopped onion
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • ½ teaspoon cumin
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • ⅛ teaspoon pepper
  • 1 (16 ounce) can refried beans
  • 1 (4.5 ounce) can diced green chile peppers
  • 1 (15 ounce) can chili without beans
  • 1 (10.5 ounce) can condensed tomato soup
  • 1 (10 ounce) can enchilada sauce
  • 6 (12 inch) flour tortillas, warmed
  • 2 cups shredded lettuce, divided
  • 1 cup chopped tomatoes, divided
  • 2 cups shredded Mexican cheese blend, divided
  • ½ cup chopped green onions, divided

Steps

  1. Step 1

    Crumble ground beef into a skillet over medium-high heat.

  2. Step 2

    Cook and stir until evenly browned.

  3. step 3

    Add onion and cook until translucent.

  4. step 4

    Drain grease, and season with garlic, cumin, salt, and pepper.

  5. step 5

    Stir in refried beans and green chilies until well blended.

  6. step 6

    Turn off heat but keep warm.

  7. Step 7

    Combine canned chili, condensed soup, and enchilada sauce in a saucepan.

  8. step 8

    Mix well and cook over medium heat until heated through.

  9. step 9

    Turn off the heat and keep warm.

  10. step 10

    Place warmed tortilla on a plate and spoon a generous 1/2 cup of the ground beef mixture onto the center.

  11. step 11

    op with a portion of lettuce and tomato to your liking. Roll up tortilla around the filling, while tucking in the sides.

  12. step 12

    Spoon a generous amount of the sauce over the top and sprinkle with a portion of cheese and green onions.

  13. Step 13

    Heat in the microwave until cheese is melted, about 30 seconds. Repeat with remaining tortillas and fillings.
